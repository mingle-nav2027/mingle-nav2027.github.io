Video gallery

Every hardware clip we recorded — 17 encounters on the Unitree G1, successes first and failures at the end, each with three views.

Views. Third-person — the external recording of the trial. First-person — the robot's chest-mounted ZED 2i RGB stream. Depth + BEV — the ZED 2i depth stacked above the history occupancy map the policy is conditioned on. The onboard views cover the interaction window, so they can be shorter than the third-person recording.
